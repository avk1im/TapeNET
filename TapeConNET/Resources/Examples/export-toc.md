## Examples

Save a spare copy of the TOC:

    tapecon export-toc D:\Safe\first.tapetoc

*Keep one for every important cartridge.* It is the rescue path if both copies on tape are lost, and the only way back
to the files of an encrypted set without its TOC.

Export the TOC of the **last** volume of a series: it also serves the earlier volumes, should their own TOC fail.

    tapecon export-toc D:\Safe\series.tapetoc
